Etsy public ecommerce search, product, seller and review data.

Authenticate with `X-API-Key`. Requests use `https://scrapebadger.com/v1/etsy`.

## Endpoints

| Endpoint | Request | Credits |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| [Search listings](/api-reference/endpoint/etsy/search) | `GET /search` | 10 |
| [Get listing detail](/api-reference/endpoint/etsy/listings-listing-id) | `GET /listings/{listing_id}` | 10 |
| [Get shop profile](/api-reference/endpoint/etsy/shops-shop-name) | `GET /shops/{shop_name}` | 10 |
| [Get shop listings](/api-reference/endpoint/etsy/shops-shop-name-listings) | `GET /shops/{shop_name}/listings` | 10 |
| [Get shop reviews](/api-reference/endpoint/etsy/shops-shop-name-reviews) | `GET /shops/{shop_name}/reviews` | 20 |
| [Get categories](/api-reference/endpoint/etsy/categories) | `GET /categories` | 10 |
| [Get listing reviews](/api-reference/endpoint/etsy/listings-listing-id-reviews) | `GET /listings/{listing_id}/reviews` | 20 |
| [Get search suggestions](/api-reference/endpoint/etsy/autocomplete) | `GET /autocomplete` | 20 |

Credits apply to successful requests. Failed requests and empty result sets are not charged.

## Markets

Supported market codes: `US`, `GB`.

Use the same market and location parameters across search, product and availability requests. Store availability and prices reflect the requested context at collection time.

## Pagination

Follow each endpoint’s pagination fields and retain product IDs to deduplicate results. Search counts and stock can change between calls.

## Get started

[Open the playground](https://scrapebadger.com/dashboard/etsy-scraper) or use the [Python and Node.js SDKs](/sdks).

## Search filters

Keyword, price, sorting and pagination controls are available on Search. The Categories endpoints provide navigation data; category filtering is not offered on Search.
