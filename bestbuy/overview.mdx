Search Best Buy US products, compare deals and specifications, read reviews and questions, and check ZIP-specific fulfillment.

Authenticate with `X-API-Key`. Requests use `https://scrapebadger.com/v1/bestbuy`.

## Endpoints

| Endpoint | Request | Credits |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| [List supported markets](/api-reference/endpoint/bestbuy/markets) | `GET /markets` | 0 |
| [Search products](/api-reference/endpoint/bestbuy/search) | `GET /search` | 10 |
| [Browse a category](/api-reference/endpoint/bestbuy/category) | `GET /category` | 10 |
| [Get categories](/api-reference/endpoint/bestbuy/categories) | `GET /categories` | 5 |
| [Get laptop and computer deals](/api-reference/endpoint/bestbuy/deals) | `GET /deals` | 10 |
| [Get search suggestions](/api-reference/endpoint/bestbuy/autocomplete) | `GET /autocomplete` | 5 |
| [Get product reviews](/api-reference/endpoint/bestbuy/products-sku-reviews) | `GET /products/{sku}/reviews` | 5 |
| [Get product questions](/api-reference/endpoint/bestbuy/products-sku-questions) | `GET /products/{sku}/questions` | 5 |
| [Get product availability](/api-reference/endpoint/bestbuy/products-sku-availability) | `GET /products/{sku}/availability` | 5 |
| [Get product detail](/api-reference/endpoint/bestbuy/products-sku) | `GET /products/{sku}` | 10 |

Credits apply to successful requests. Failed requests and empty result sets are not charged.

## Markets

Supported market codes: `us`.

Use the same market and location parameters across search, product and availability requests. Store availability and prices reflect the requested context at collection time.

## Pagination

Follow each endpoint’s pagination fields and retain product IDs to deduplicate results. Search counts and stock can change between calls.
