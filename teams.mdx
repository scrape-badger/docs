Teams let a company use one ScrapeBadger balance from several user accounts. Each member signs in with their own account, and the team's API keys draw credits from the **team owner's** balance and use the owner's rate limit.

Create a team on the [Teams](https://scrapebadger.com/dashboard/teams) page.

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
|------|------------------|
| **Owner** | Everything. Pays for the team's usage. The only one who can invite or manage admins, and delete the team. Can't leave the team. |
| **Admin** | Invite and remove members, change their permissions, and manage every team key. |
| **Member** | See the team's keys, plus whatever an admin grants them (below). |

If the owner [deletes their account](/delete-account), the team is deleted with it, including every member's team API keys.

## Member permissions

Admins grant plain members any of these:

| Permission | Allows |
|------------|--------|
| **Create API keys** | Create their own member keys. |
| **Delete any key** | Disable or delete any member's key. |
| **Manage service keys** | Create, edit, view and delete the team's service keys. |

A member can always edit, view, disable and delete the keys they created themselves.

## Member keys and service keys

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Member keys" icon="user">
    Belong to the member who created them. They stop working the moment that member leaves or is removed from the team. Nobody else — not even the owner — can view a member's key.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Service keys" icon="robot">
    Owned by the team, not a person, so they keep working when people come and go. Use them for production systems. Only members with **Manage service keys** can view or change them.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Both kinds support notes, expiry dates, [API permissions](/authentication#api-key-permissions) and [IP restrictions](/authentication#ip-restrictions), exactly like personal keys. A team key calling an API it isn't permitted to call gets `403 insufficient_scope`.

## Inviting people

Admins invite by email from the team's **Members** tab, choosing the role and, for members, their permissions. The invitee gets an email with a link that:

- works only for the invited email address, on an account whose email is verified
- expires after 7 days
- can be revoked from the pending invitations list

Invitees can also accept from the [Teams](https://scrapebadger.com/dashboard/teams) page once they're signed in. Inviting the same email again replaces the earlier invitation.

## Single sign-on (SSO)

Connect your company's identity provider to a team, and anyone with an email on your company domain can sign in with it and join the team automatically. Any OpenID Connect (OIDC) provider works, including Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace and Auth0. SSO covers signing in to the dashboard only. API calls still authenticate with [API keys](/authentication).

Owners and admins set it up on the team's **Single sign-on** tab:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an app in your identity provider">
    Create an **OIDC web application** that uses the authorization code grant. You can create it with any redirect URI and update it in step 3. ScrapeBadger requests the `openid`, `email` and `profile` scopes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the app's details">
    Enter your email domain (for example `example.com`), the **issuer URL** (in Okta, `https://<your-org>.okta.com`, or your custom authorization server's issuer), the **client ID** and the **client secret**. ScrapeBadger reads the issuer's discovery document to find its endpoints.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the redirect URI to the app">
    Copy the **Redirect URI** shown on the tab into the app's sign-in redirect URIs. It looks like `https://scrapebadger.com/api/auth/sso/callback/sso-example-com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the domain">
    Add the DNS TXT record shown on the tab at your DNS provider, then click **Verify domain**:

    | Type | Name | Value |
    |------|------|-------|
    | TXT | `_scrapebadger-sso.example.com` | `scrapebadger-sso-verification=…` |

    Nobody can sign in through the provider until the domain is verified. This stops anyone outside your company from putting an identity provider in front of your addresses.
  </Step>
</Steps>

From then on, people choose **Sign in with SSO** on the [sign-in page](https://scrapebadger.com/auth/signin) and enter their work email:

- **New people** get an account the first time they sign in, and join the team as members. Grant them permissions from the **Members** tab as usual.
- **Existing accounts** on your domain are linked the first time their owner signs in through SSO. They can keep using their password or Google sign-in too.
- Only addresses on the verified domain and its subdomains can sign in through it. If the identity provider returns any other address, the sign-in is refused.

A team can have one identity provider, for one domain, and a domain can belong to only one team. Webmail domains such as `gmail.com` can't be used. To rotate the client secret, enter the new one on the tab. **Turn off** removes the connection. Members stay in the team, but anyone who only ever signed in through SSO has no password, so they'll need **Continue with Google** on the same email (or support) to get back in.

## Audit log

Owners and admins see an **Audit log** tab on the team page: a permanent, read-only record of who did what and when.

| Category | Events recorded |
|----------|-----------------|
| Team | team created, renamed |
| Invitations | sent (with role and permissions), revoked, accepted, declined |
| Members | role or permission changes (before → after), removed, left, joined through single sign-on |
| Single sign-on | set up, settings changed (incl. secret rotation), domain verified, turned off |
| API keys | created, edited (each changed field, before → after, including API permissions), enabled, disabled, deleted — also when a member's keys are deleted because they left — and **every time a key's secret was viewed** |

Each entry shows the person who acted, the time, and the IP address it came from. Filter by category, and expand an entry to see the exact changes. Entries cannot be edited or deleted; they are removed only if the team itself is deleted.

## Leaving and deleting

- A member or admin can leave from the team's **Settings** tab. Their member keys are deleted; service keys are unaffected.
- The owner can delete the team, which immediately and permanently deletes every team key.
