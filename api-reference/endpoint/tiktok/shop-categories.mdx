---
title: "Shop Root Categories"
openapi: "GET /v1/tiktok/shop/categories"
description: "The 28 top-level TikTok Shop categories of a market — drill down with the category endpoint."
---

<Note>
  Returns the 28 top-level categories with their ids (shared across markets). Pass an id to [Shop Category Products](/api-reference/endpoint/tiktok/shop-category) for subcategories and top products.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Markets** — `region=US` (default) serves everything; `GB` and `ID` (Tokopedia) serve categories, category products and product detail in GBP/IDR. SG, MY, PH, TH, VN, MX, BR and JP sit behind a puzzle our exits do not clear and return `400`.
</Note>

<Note>
  Each request costs **5 credits**. Failed requests are not charged.
</Note>
