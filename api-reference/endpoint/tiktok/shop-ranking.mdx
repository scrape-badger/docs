---
title: "Shop Best Sellers"
openapi: "GET /v1/tiktok/shop/ranking"
description: "TikTok Shop's official best sellers of the past 30 days, ranked, with price, discount, rating, exact sold count and seller."
---

<Note>
  The storefront's own **"Best sellers in past 30 days"** ranking (about 20 products) — `rank` is the 1-based position; deal labels such as `Limited time deal` sit in `labels`. US only.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Markets** — `region=US` (default) serves everything; `GB` and `ID` (Tokopedia) serve categories, category products and product detail in GBP/IDR. SG, MY, PH, TH, VN, MX, BR and JP sit behind a puzzle our exits do not clear and return `400`.
</Note>

<Note>
  Each request costs **5 credits**. Failed requests are not charged.
</Note>
